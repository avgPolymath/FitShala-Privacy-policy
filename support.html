Frequently Asked Questions

My workout plan isn't loading?

Please ensure you have an active internet connection. Try pulling down on the home screen to refresh. If the issue persists, log out and log back in.

How do I swap an exercise?

In the active workout screen, tap the "Swap" icon next to any exercise to find a suitable alternative based on your available equipment.

Data & Privacy

Request Account Deletion

We respect your right to be forgotten. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the FitShala App.
  2. Go to Settings (Profile Icon).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your choice. Your data will be wiped immediately.

Alternatively, you can email us at fitshala.ai@gmail.com with the subject "DELETE ACCOUNT" and your registered phone number.
